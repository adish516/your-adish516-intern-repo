What I Want to Learn and Contribute

What do you hope to learn from this internship?

I want to see how marketing and partnerships actually work outside of uni. We learn a lot of theory, but I want to understand how you find the right people to work with, approach them and build something from that. I also want to understand what working in a startup is like.

What skills are you most excited to develop?

I’m interested in getting better at researching potential partners and figuring out how to approach them. I want to write messages that feel personal and give people a reason to respond. I also want to learn how to tell whether the work I’m doing is actually helping.

What do you think you can contribute to the team?

I’ve helped with emails, contracts and coordinating things in my family’s business, so I have some experience with communicating and following up.

I also enjoy photography and making content for social media, so I’d be keen to contribute creative ideas where they’re useful. Having studied psychology and now international business, I’m interested in both understanding people and how a business reaches them. 

What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?

Probably reaching out to people I don’t know in a professional setting. Sometimes I overthink what to say or whether I should follow up. I’d like to get more comfortable with that through practice and feedback.

What does success in this internship look like for you?

I want to feel like I’ve actually helped the team and learned something I can use afterwards. That could be finding suitable partners, helping with outreach or contributing an idea that gets used. I don’t expect to know everything by the end, but I’d like to feel more
confident doing the work and taking initiative without needing guidance for every step.
