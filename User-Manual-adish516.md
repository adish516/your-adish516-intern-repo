# User Manual for Adish

## A bit about me

I’m Adish, and I’m studying a Master of International Business at the
University of Melbourne. I previously studied psychology, and I’m joining
Focus Bear as a Partnership Marketing Intern.

Outside of uni and work, I enjoy photography, creating content, music
and going to concerts.

## What helps me do my best work

Clear expectations help me a lot, especially when something is new.
It’s useful to know what the task is, why we’re doing it, when it’s
needed and what a good result would look like.

An example helps too. Once I understand the task, I like having space
to work through it and ask questions when needed.

## How I prefer to communicate

Messages work well for everyday questions and updates because I can
refer back to them while working. Emails work too, I am usually very quick with my replies during weekdays.

## My availability

My planned working days are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday,
usually from 2:00–4:30 pm Melbourne time, working remotely.

If a meeting needs to happen outside those hours, advance notice helps
me plan around other commitments. I’ll communicate if my availability
changes.

## What helps me stay focused

It helps to have a clear list of tasks and know which one matters most.
Breaking a bigger task into smaller steps makes it easier to get started.

I’m using Focus Bear sessions to track my internship work. Having some
uninterrupted time helps me make progress, but I also want to raise
questions early rather than spend too long stuck.

## How I like to receive feedback

I appreciate feedback that’s clear and specific. If something needs
changing, it helps to know which part, why and what I could do differently.

I also like knowing what worked well so I can keep doing it. Written
feedback is useful because I can refer back to it while making changes.
For anything more involved, I’m happy to talk it through.

## How I give feedback

I try to be respectful and focus on the work rather than the person.
I’d explain what I noticed and suggest an improvement instead of just
saying something isn’t good.

I’m also open to hearing the reasoning behind someone’s approach,
because there may be context I’ve missed.

## Things to know about my work style

I can sometimes overthink how to word a message, especially when
reaching out to someone new. Having clear expectations around
communication and follow-ups helps.

I’m still getting familiar with tools like GitHub, so I might need
clarification on a process that’s familiar to others. I want to
understand what I’m doing rather than just follow steps without
knowing why.

I also enjoy the creative side of work and would be keen to contribute
ideas around content, messaging and reaching different audiences.

## If something isn’t working

Please let me know directly and respectfully rather than assume I’ve
picked up on it. I’d rather clarify something early and fix it.

I’ll try to do the same by being honest about progress, asking for
help when I need it and letting the team know if a deadline is at risk.
