What I’ll work on

Initially, I’ll use these sessions for onboarding, learning the product and completing the required reflections and practical tasks.

As I move into my role, I’ll use the time for partner research, outreach drafts, meetings, feedback and other tasks agreed with my supervisor.

Tracking my hours

I’ll start a Focus Bear focus session whenever I work on internship tasks and stop it when I take a break or do something unrelated.

I’ll check my timesheet each Friday to compare my actual hours with this plan. If I miss a session or finish early, I’ll arrange additional time rather than count hours I haven’t worked.

Any required meetings outside these afternoon sessions will be recorded as actual internship time, without double-counting them.

Adjustments

This is my proposed schedule. I’ll confirm the company’s holiday arrangements and meeting expectations with my supervisor.

If travel, appointments or exam commitments affect my availability, I’ll communicate early and update the plan. I’ll aim to complete a few extra hours before the final week so I’m not relying on the small margin above 90 hours.
